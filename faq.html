@@ privacy & data @@

No server sees your code. Pyor talks to GitHub directly from your machine using your own credentials. We do run a backend, but it handles only sign-in, billing, and your team’s seats — it never receives, stores, or proxies your code, diffs, or pull requests. Those flow between your device and GitHub, never through us.
